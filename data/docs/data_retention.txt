Nimbus Cloud Storage Data Retention and Deletion

When a user deletes a file from Nimbus, the file is not immediately purged.
Instead, it moves to a soft-delete state and remains recoverable from the
account's Trash for 30 days. Files in the Trash count against the account's
storage quota. After 30 days, files are automatically and permanently purged
from Nimbus's storage systems and cannot be recovered by Nimbus support
under any circumstances.

If a customer cancels their subscription or deletes their entire account,
all account data, including files, metadata, and activity logs, is retained
in a soft-deleted state for 30 days in case the customer wants to reactivate
the account. During this window, reactivation restores full access to all
data exactly as it was at the time of deletion. After the 30 day window
closes, all account data is permanently and irreversibly purged, including
from backup systems, within an additional 7 days.

Customers who want to preserve their data before deleting an account should
use the account export tool, available from account settings, which
packages all files and metadata into a downloadable archive. Exports are
generated asynchronously and the customer is emailed a download link once
the archive is ready, typically within a few hours for accounts under 1TB.

Enterprise customers with regulatory retention requirements can configure
a custom retention policy that extends the soft-delete window up to 365
days, or, alternatively, a legal hold that prevents permanent deletion of
specified files entirely until the hold is manually released by an
authorized admin. Legal holds override the standard 30 day purge timeline
and are logged in the account's audit trail.

Backups of the storage system itself are retained separately from
customer-facing Trash and are kept for disaster recovery purposes for 90
days, but backup data is not accessible to customers or support staff for
individual file recovery outside of the standard 30 day Trash window.
