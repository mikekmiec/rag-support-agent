Nimbus Cloud Storage Pricing

Nimbus offers three plans: Starter, Team, and Enterprise. The Starter plan is
$9 per month and includes 500GB of storage, 3 team seats, and standard
support with a 24 hour response time. The Team plan is $29 per month and
includes 2TB of storage, 15 team seats, priority support, and versioned file
history going back 90 days. The Enterprise plan is custom-priced and includes
unlimited storage, single sign-on, a dedicated account manager, and a
guaranteed uptime SLA.

Customers who pay annually instead of monthly receive a 20% discount on the
Starter and Team plans. The discount is applied automatically at checkout
when the annual billing option is selected and cannot be combined with other
promotional codes. Enterprise contracts are negotiated individually and are
not eligible for the standard annual discount, though volume discounts are
available for commitments over 50 seats.

Storage overage fees apply once a plan's included storage is exceeded.
Starter and Team customers are billed $0.10 per additional GB per month,
prorated daily. Overage charges appear as a separate line item on the
following month's invoice. Customers can set a storage cap in account
settings to prevent overage charges entirely; once the cap is reached,
new uploads are rejected with an error until storage is freed up or the
cap is raised.

Refunds are issued only within the first 14 days of a new subscription and
only for annual plans. Monthly plans can be canceled at any time from the
billing page, and cancellation takes effect at the end of the current
billing cycle. There are no early termination fees on any plan, including
Enterprise contracts, provided 30 days written notice is given.

All prices are listed in USD. Customers on annual plans are billed once per
year on the anniversary of their signup date. A 7 day grace period applies
after a failed payment before storage access is suspended, during which
Nimbus will retry the charge and send email reminders to the account's
billing contact.
