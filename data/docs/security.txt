Nimbus Cloud Storage Security

All files stored in Nimbus are encrypted at rest using AES-256, and all data
in transit between the client and Nimbus servers is encrypted using TLS 1.3.
Encryption keys are managed by Nimbus's internal key management service and
are rotated automatically every 90 days. Customers on the Enterprise plan can
opt into bringing their own encryption keys through the customer-managed key
feature.

Account login is protected by a failed-attempt lockout policy. After 5
consecutive failed login attempts, an account is locked for 30 minutes. The
lockout timer resets after a successful login. During a lockout, the account
owner receives an email notification with a link to reset their password
immediately, which also clears the lockout early. This policy applies to all
plans and cannot be disabled, even on Enterprise accounts.

Two-factor authentication (2FA) is available on all plans and is strongly
recommended. 2FA can be configured using an authenticator app (TOTP) or SMS,
though SMS-based 2FA is being deprecated and will be removed in a future
release. Enterprise customers can enforce mandatory 2FA for all users in
their organization through the admin console, and can also configure single
sign-on (SSO) via SAML 2.0 with providers such as Okta and Azure AD.

Nimbus undergoes an annual third-party security audit and maintains SOC 2
Type II compliance. Audit reports are available to Team and Enterprise
customers upon request through the account's designated security contact.
Nimbus does not sell customer data to third parties and does not use
customer file contents to train any machine learning models.

Suspicious login activity, such as a login from a new country or device,
triggers an automatic email alert to the account owner. Enterprise admins
can additionally configure IP allowlisting to restrict account access to a
specific set of corporate network ranges, and can view a full audit log of
every login and file access event for the past 12 months.
