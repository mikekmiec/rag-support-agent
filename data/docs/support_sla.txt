Nimbus Cloud Storage Support SLA

Support response times vary by plan tier. Starter plan customers receive
standard email support with a target first-response time of 24 hours during
business days. Team plan customers receive priority support with a target
first-response time of 4 hours, 7 days a week, and gain access to live chat
during business hours in addition to email.

Enterprise customers receive a guaranteed first-response SLA of 1 hour for
issues classified as Critical (complete service outage or data loss risk),
4 hours for High severity issues (major feature broken, no workaround), and
1 business day for Normal severity issues (minor bugs, general questions).
Enterprise support is available 24/7/365 via phone, email, and a dedicated
Slack Connect channel with the customer's assigned account team.

If Nimbus fails to meet the Enterprise first-response SLA for a Critical
issue, the customer is automatically credited 5% of their monthly contract
value for that billing cycle, up to a maximum credit of 30% in any single
month. SLA credits are applied automatically to the next invoice and do not
require the customer to file a claim, though customers can view SLA
compliance history in the Enterprise admin console.

Nimbus's platform status page publishes real-time uptime data and incident
history for all core services, including the API, file upload/download
pipeline, and web dashboard. Enterprise customers can subscribe to
proactive incident notifications via email, SMS, or a webhook that fires
whenever an incident is opened, updated, or resolved on the status page.

Feature requests and non-urgent product feedback should be submitted
through the community forum rather than the support SLA channels, since
the SLA is reserved for issues affecting the customer's ability to use the
service. The product team reviews forum feedback monthly but does not
guarantee a response timeline for feature requests.
